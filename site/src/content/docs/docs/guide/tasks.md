## Views

| View | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Inbox** | Tasks without a list |
| **Today** | Tasks due today, and overdue tasks |
| **Upcoming** | Tasks due after today |
| **Done** | Completed tasks |

Under **Lists**, **New list** creates a list. Each list has its own view. **Delete list** removes the list and all its tasks.

## Add and complete

Type in **Add a task, then press Enter**. Tick the box to mark a task as done; tick it again to reopen it.

## Edit

Click a task to open it. You can set:

- a **description**, in Markdown;
- a **due date** and a **time**;
- a **priority**: none, low, medium or high;
- **labels**, separated by commas;
- the **list** it belongs to;
- **subtasks**, with **Add a subtask**.

Tasks with a due date also appear in the **Calendar**. Tasks made from a note checklist show **From note** with a link to the note.

**Delete task** removes the task and its subtasks.
