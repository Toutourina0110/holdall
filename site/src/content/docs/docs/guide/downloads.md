## Download

1. Paste one or more links in **Links**, one per line: videos, playlists or channels.
2. **Check links** reads each link and shows what it contains.
3. For each video, choose:
   - **Video** or **Audio only**;
   - the **format** and **quality** (**Best available** by default), or the **audio format**;
   - or open **Available formats** to pick an exact one.
4. **Download** starts it. For a playlist, **Download all** queues every video.

## Queue

The **Queue** shows each download as **Waiting**, **Downloading**, **Done**, **Failed** or **Paused**, with the time left.

- **Pause** and **Resume** a download;
- **Try again** after a failure;
- **Save file** downloads the result to your device;
- **Remove from the list** hides a finished entry.

Finished files are also in **Files**, in the **Downloads** folder.

## Download tools

Holdall uses yt-dlp to download and ffmpeg to merge and convert. **Download tools** shows their versions.

- On a server, they come with the Docker image. Update the image to update them.
- On the desktop app, **Install or update** gets the latest yt-dlp. It is also updated automatically.

Websites change often. When a download fails on a site that used to work, update yt-dlp first.
