---
title: Your account
description: Change your password and sign out other devices.
---

Open **Settings, Account**.

- **Profile** shows your name, email and role.
- **Password**: enter your **current password** and a **new password** of at least 10 characters, then **Change password**. Your other devices are signed out.
- **Signed-in devices** lists every browser and app signed in to your account. **Sign out** ends one session; **Sign out other devices** ends all of them except this one.

## Forgot your password

Holdall sends no email. Ask an admin: in **Admin, People**, they choose **Reset password** next to your name and send you the link themselves. The link works once, for 60 minutes. Open it, choose a new password, then sign in. You are signed out on every device.

On the desktop app you are signed in automatically.
