## Create the admin account

On a new server, the first visit opens **Set up Holdall**. Enter your name, email and a password of at least 10 characters. This account is the **admin**: it can invite people and install plugins. The setup page stops working as soon as this account exists.

The desktop app skips this step: it creates a local account and signs you in.

## Invite people

Open **Admin** in the side bar.

1. Under **Invitations**, choose **Member** or **Admin**, then **Create invitation link**.
2. Copy the link and send it yourself, by message or email. Holdall sends no email.
3. The link works once and expires after 7 days. It is shown only once: anyone who has it can create an account. **Revoke** cancels an open invitation.

Under **Registration**, **Open to anyone** lets anyone who reaches your Holdall create a member account without an invitation. **Invitation only** is the default.

Under **People**, change someone's role between **Member** and **Admin**.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Member | Use every module with their own data, customize their own layout and theme, turn installed plugins on or off. |
| Admin | Everything a member can, plus invite people, change roles, open registration, install and remove plugins, add CalDAV servers on the local network. |

Nobody, not even an admin, can open another person's notes, tasks, events or files.

## Find your way around

The side bar has three groups:

- **Today**, **Notes**, **Tasks**, **Calendar**;
- **Media and files**: **Downloads**, **Files**, **Converter**;
- **System**: **Plugins**, **Settings**, **Admin** (admins only).

Plugins can add their own pages to the side bar. On a phone, the main pages move to a bar at the bottom of the screen.
