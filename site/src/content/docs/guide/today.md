---
title: Today
description: The start page, made of widgets from modules and plugins.
---

**Today** is the page Holdall opens on. It shows widgets:

- tasks due today, with a link to **Tasks**;
- today's events, with a link to **Calendar**;
- downloads in progress;
- widgets added by plugins you turned on.

To choose which widgets appear and in what order, use **Customize** in the top bar (see [Customize](/holdall/guide/customize/)).
